A Letter to Preethi

Read this slowly. Every line was typed with your face in my mind.

Dear Preethi,

I don’t know the exact moment it happened, but somewhere between our random conversations and your quiet little smiles, my heart decided that you were its favourite place to be.

You have this gentle way of making everything feel lighter — even on days when I don’t say a word about what’s going on in my head. You notice things, you remember the small details, and somehow you always know how to make me feel a little more okay.

I love the way you exist in my day: sometimes loud, sometimes soft, but always there in the back of my mind like a song I never get tired of. You turn ordinary moments into memories I want to keep replaying.

This little website is my way of telling you what my words never fully manage to say out loud — that you are special to me in a way that feels rare and real, and that my heart has already chosen you a thousand times over.

Whatever your answer is, I just wanted you to know this: you are deeply appreciated, genuinely admired, and quietly loved by me.

Always,

Someone whose world is softer because of you
