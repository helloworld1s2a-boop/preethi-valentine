Our Story

Not a fairytale, not a movie script — just us, imperfect and somehow still magical.

Chapter 1

The First Time I Noticed You

I still remember the first time my eyes found you and refused to look away. Something about your smile made the room softer, and my day a little lighter.

Chapter 2

Little Moments, Quietly Loud

The way you laugh at the smallest things, the way you say my name, the way you listen even when I ramble — those moments stitched themselves into me.

Chapter 3

When I Realised

One day I caught myself talking about you like you were already home to me. That was the moment I knew my heart had quietly chosen you long ago.

Chapter 4

Today

Today, I am here, turning all these unspoken feelings into words and code, hoping this small universe I built shows you at least a fraction of what you mean to me.

Every chapter has one constant: you.
