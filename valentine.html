A Tiny Big Question

Preethi, will you be my Valentine?

Preethi,

Thank you for saying yes — or at least for coming this far with me in this little universe I built for you. Somewhere between every click and every scroll, I just wanted you to feel how important you are to me.

Choosing you is the easiest thing my heart does every single day. I don’t love you in some loud, dramatic way; I love you in the way I look forward to your messages, in the way my mood lifts when I see your name, in the way I imagine my future and quietly hope you are in it.

You are, to me, a safe place and a beautiful possibility at the same time. And if you’ll let me, I’d like to keep choosing you — not just on Valentine’s Day, but on all the ordinary days in between.

With all my heart,

Me, hopelessly, happily, yours.

No matter how many pages I make, "you" will always be my favourite one.
